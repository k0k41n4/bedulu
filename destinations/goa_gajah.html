9th Century

Elephant Cave

History & Philosophy

The Goa Gajah site is an archaeological wonder that blends Hindu and Buddhist elements. The giant carving at the cave's mouth represents the face of Bhoma, whose function is to ward off evil spirits and maintain the sanctity of this hermitage.

Inside, a T-shaped passageway houses statues of Ganesha and Lingga-Yoni, demonstrating the high level of spiritual tolerance during the reign of King Udayana.

Destination Analysis (4A)

Attraction

  • Primary: 9th century Elephant Cave, Ganesha Statue, & Lingga-Yoni.
  • Secondary: Relief of river cliffs & ancient lightning pools.

Accessibility

  • Location: Main route of Jl. Raya Goa Gajah (Strategic).
  • Condition: Smooth asphalt road access, large buses can access.
  • Mobility: The steps are quite steep to the river area.

Amenity

  • Facilities: Toilets, Large Parking Area, & Souvenir Kiosk.
  • Culinary: Local cafes and restaurants around the parking area.

Ancillary

  • Management: Gianyar Tourism Office & Bedulu Traditional Village.
  • Services: Information Post, Local Guide, & Digital Ticket Service.

Facility & Tips

  • Clothes rental (included in ticket)
  • Licensed local guide
  • Spacious parking area and clean restrooms
  • Best time: 8:00 AM - 10:00 AM WITA
