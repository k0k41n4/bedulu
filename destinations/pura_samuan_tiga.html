Agung Diplomacy

Pura Samuan Tiga

History & Philosophy

Samuan Tiga Temple is a silent witness to the great diplomacy that united the various Hindu sects in Bali into a harmonious order during the reign of King Udayana Warmadewa. The name "Samuan Tiga" itself means "Three Meetings," referring to the great meeting that resulted in the concept of the Traditional Village and Kahyangan Tiga in Bali.

Philosophically, this temple symbolizes peace and unity. Its unique architecture, with seven levels of courtyards (Mandala), demonstrates a profound cosmic structure rarely found in other temples in Bali.

Destination Analysis (4A)

Attraction

  • Cultural: The unique and world-famous Siat Sampian ritual.
  • Architecture: The magnificent and historic structure of the 7 Mandalas.

Accessibility

  • Location: Located right in the center of Bedulu Village.
  • Access: Very easy to reach by private vehicle or tour bus.

Amenity

  • Facilities: Spacious wantilan, toilets, and parking area in front of the temple.
  • Surrounding Area: Close to local food stalls typical of Gianyar.

Ancillary

  • Organization: Strictly managed by the Bedulu Traditional Village.
  • Info: There is a historical information board in the front area.

Visitor Ethics

  • It is mandatory to wear Balinese cloth (senteng/sarong).
  • Entry is prohibited for women who are menstruating (Cuntaka).
  • Maintain peace as the area is an active place of worship.
  • Best time to visit: Afternoon or during odalan ceremonies.
