Yeh Pulu

History & Philosophy

Hidden within the green rice paddies of Bedulu Village, Yeh Pulu houses an iconic 25-meter-long sandstone relief gallery. Rediscovered in 1925, this site represents a legacy of the Majapahit Kingdom in Bali.

Unlike reliefs on other temples, which typically tell the stories of the Ramayana or Mahabharata, Yeh Pulu explicitly depicts the daily lives of ancient Balinese people: hunting, farming, and social interactions. This is the most honest historical portrait of the Bedulu civilization of the past.

Destination Analysis (4A)

Attraction

  • Primary: 25m high rock relief & unique Ganesha statue.
  • Secondary: View of terraced rice fields & holy springs.

Accessibility

  • Location: Enter through a quiet village road.
  • Soft Adventure: You need to walk along the rice field embankment for about 200m.

Amenity

  • Facilities: Ticket counter, restrooms, & rest area.
  • Advantages: Very quiet, beautiful environment (hidden gem).

Ancillary

  • Status: Under the protection of the BPK (Cultural Preservation Agency).
  • Guide: Usually guided by a local site guard.

Visitor Tips

  • Wear comfortable footwear as you will be walking through rice paddies.
  • Bring your own drinking water, as the kiosk is quite far from the relief.
  • Best time: Early morning (8:00 AM) to get the right sunlight for photographing the relief.

Explore a Different Side of Bedulu

Yeh Pulu offers a serenity you won't find in other crowded tourist destinations.
